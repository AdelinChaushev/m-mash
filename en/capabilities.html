Precision Machining Tailored to Customer Requirements

By applying appropriate machining technologies, we strive to achieve the required geometry, dimensional accuracy and surface quality in accordance with the specifications of each component.

We take an individual approach to every project, considering the component’s design, material, intended function and agreed technical parameters.

CNC Turning

CNC turning is a key technology in the production of rotational components. Computer-controlled machining operations are used to manufacture cylindrical and other rotationally symmetrical components in accordance with the specified geometry and technical documentation.

Production is organized according to the requirements of each component, with particular attention to dimensions, tolerances, functional surfaces and process repeatability.

Milling and Mechanical Machining

Milling and other applicable machining operations enable the production of various geometric features and functional surfaces on components.

The manufacturing approach is selected according to the material, geometry, required accuracy and specific characteristics of the part. The objective is to achieve conformity with the technical drawing and the requirements of the component’s intended application.

Grinding and Finishing

Grinding is a precision machining process used when the component design and technical requirements call for higher dimensional accuracy or a specified surface finish.

Depending on the component and the available equipment, grinding may be used to process cylindrical or flat surfaces and achieve the required dimensions and surface characteristics.

Finishing operations are important for components where surface contact, assembly requirements and functional performance demand precise execution.

Taken Into Account During Production

  • Specified tolerances
  • Surface roughness requirements
  • Applicable acceptance criteria

Manufacturing to Technical Drawings and Individual Specifications

Every manufacturing project begins with a review of the technical documentation and the customer’s requirements. Based on this information, the applicable manufacturing operations and feasibility of the order are assessed.

We manufacture components based on technical drawings, samples and individual specifications, aiming to ensure consistency between the design requirements and the finished product.

This approach enables our customers to outsource the production of components to a specialized manufacturing partner that understands the importance of technical documentation, quality and reliability in industrial supply chains.

Our Approach to Manufacturing

At M-MASH Ltd., every order is assessed according to its specific characteristics and requirements. We strive to combine efficient production planning, rational use of resources and a consistent approach to quality.

Our goal is to build long-term partnerships with industrial companies by providing manufacturing solutions tailored to the technical specifications of their components and their operational needs.
