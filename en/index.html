Your Drawing. Our Manufacturing Expertise.

M-MASH Ltd. is a Bulgarian manufacturing partner specializing in the production of metal components based on technical drawings, samples and individual customer requirements.

Combining manufacturing experience, machining expertise and a flexible approach, we work to provide industrial companies with reliable supplies of components that meet their technical requirements.

From selecting the appropriate manufacturing process to producing the finished component, our goal is to deliver reliable results and build long-term partnerships.

Project “Technological Modernization of the Enterprise” 2023 · National Recovery and Resilience Plan · Contract No. BG-RRP-3.004-0388-C01 ShowHide

The project is carried out by the final recipient, M-MASH Ltd., under the National Recovery and Resilience Plan, Priority Axis “Smart Industry”, procedure BG-RRP-3.004 “Technological Modernization”.

On 10.04.2023, M-MASH Ltd. began implementing the project “Technological Modernization of the Enterprise” under Financing Agreement No. BG-RRP-3.004-0388-C01, procedure BG-RRP-3.004 “Technological Modernization”.

The project will be carried out in Kazanlak with financial support from the EU under NextGenerationEU through the National Recovery and Resilience Plan. The planned duration is 12 months.

The aim of the project is to provide focused support to Bulgarian small and medium-sized enterprises to increase their competitiveness and improve their market position. The project aims to achieve the following specific objectives:

  • Digitalization of the production process by optimizing production processes, as a result of the automated functions of the lathes and the programmed setting of the individual stages.
  • Expansion of production capacity through the greater capacity of the lathes, shorter production time per component and greater precision, and by adding this capacity to the existing facilities.
