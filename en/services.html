Manufacturing Solutions Tailored to Your Requirements

As a subcontract manufacturing partner, M-MASH strives to provide suitable technical solutions that take into account the intended application of each component, quality requirements and the needs of industrial production.

Combining manufacturing experience with specialized machining expertise, we work with customers seeking a reliable partner for metal component production and long-term manufacturing cooperation.

Our Services

Metal Component Manufacturing to Drawings

We manufacture metal components based on customer-provided technical documentation and agreed requirements. Each project is assessed individually, taking into account the component’s geometry, material, dimensions and intended application.

CNC Turning and Mechanical Machining

We provide machining services using applicable manufacturing technologies and available equipment. Our objective is to achieve the required dimensions, functionality and quality in accordance with the technical specifications.

Rotational Component Manufacturing

We specialize in manufacturing rotational components from steel, cast iron and non-ferrous metals. Production is organized according to the component’s design characteristics and customer requirements.

Hydraulic Components

We manufacture hydraulic components in line with our product specialization and the technical requirements of each specific application.

Subcontract Manufacturing

We work with industrial companies seeking an external manufacturing partner for metal component production. We focus on clear communication, alignment of technical requirements and sustainable business cooperation.

Solutions Tailored to Your Project

Whether you have a completed technical drawing or are looking for a manufacturing partner for a specific metal component, the first step is to discuss the requirements, material, quantities and necessary characteristics.

Our approach focuses on understanding the project and identifying a suitable manufacturing solution within our actual technological capabilities.
