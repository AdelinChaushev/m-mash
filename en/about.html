Manufacturing Experience. Technical Precision. Long-Term Partnerships.

M-MASH Ltd. was established in 2005 in Kazanlak, Central Bulgaria, as a privately owned manufacturing company specializing in the production of rotational metal components made from steel, cast iron and non-ferrous metals, as well as hydraulic components.

The company’s core activity is the manufacture of rotational components based on technical drawings, customer-provided samples and individual requirements. As an established subcontract manufacturing partner for companies in Bulgaria and across Europe, we combine extensive experience, technical expertise and a flexible approach to deliver quality products, components and assemblies tailored to the specific requirements of each project.

We strive to offer competitive terms, efficient production and lead times that meet the needs of our partners.

Our Approach

  • Manufacturing based on technical drawings and customer-provided samples.
  • Selection of the appropriate manufacturing process for each component.
  • Clear communication regarding technical requirements and specifications.
  • A strong focus on quality and long-term partnerships.

Continuous Development and a Customer-Oriented Approach

M-MASH Ltd. continuously develops and improves its operations, aiming to respond to the evolving requirements of the industrial market. We value flexibility, innovation and an individual approach to every customer, while striving to build long-term and sustainable business relationships.

By optimizing production resources, making effective use of accumulated knowledge and continuously improving our working processes, we aim to increase productivity and provide reliable solutions tailored to our partners’ requirements.

Quality and Continuous Improvement

  1. 2005M-MASH Ltd. established in Kazanlak
  2. 2017Development of the quality management system begins
  3. TodayValid certification to ISO 9001:2015

Quality is a fundamental principle of M-MASH Ltd.’s operations. Since 2017, the company has been developing its quality management system and currently holds valid certification to the international standard ISO 9001:2015.

We apply a systematic approach to managing production processes, focusing on meeting agreed requirements, using resources efficiently and continuously improving our operations.

We strive to maintain consistent quality in the metal components we manufacture, taking into account the specific technical requirements and expectations of each customer.

For us, quality is more than a standard. It is the foundation for long-term partnerships built on trust, professionalism and responsibility.

Our primary goal is to continue developing, improving our manufacturing capabilities and meeting our customers’ expectations.
